<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="step-by-step-wix-migration-process">Step-by-Step Wix Migration Process&nbsp;</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Audit Your Current Website&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="margin-top:0;margin-right:0;margin-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--50);margin-left:0">Export all URLs, check traffic pages, and&nbsp;identify&nbsp;high-performing content. This prevents losing SEO value.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. Backup Content&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="margin-top:0;margin-right:0;margin-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--50);margin-left:0">Save all content, images, blog posts, and metadata before migration.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. Plan URL Structure&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="margin-top:0;margin-right:0;margin-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--50);margin-left:0">Keep URLs similar or map redirects to avoid ranking loss.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">4. Implement 301 Redirects&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="margin-top:0;margin-right:0;margin-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--50);margin-left:0">Redirect old Wix URLs to new custom URLs to preserve SEO authority.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">5.&nbsp;Optimize&nbsp;New Website&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="margin-top:0;margin-right:0;margin-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--50);margin-left:0">Improve page speed, mobile UX, schema markup, and technical SEO for better rankings.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">6.&nbsp;Submit&nbsp;to Google Search Console&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="margin-top:0;margin-right:0;margin-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--50);margin-left:0">Re-submit sitemap and monitor errors after migration.&nbsp;</p>
