<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="pro-level-cms-migration-tips-e-e-a-t-insights">Pro-Level CMS Migration Tips (E-E-A-T Insights)&nbsp;</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-ast-global-color-1-color has-text-color has-link-color wp-elements-15">1.&nbsp;Don’t&nbsp;Change Everything at Once&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Changing CMS + design + structure =&nbsp;high risk.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">👉&nbsp;Keep one variable stable.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-ast-global-color-1-color has-text-color has-link-color wp-elements-16">2. Preserve Top Pages First&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your top 20% pages drive 80% traffic.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">👉&nbsp;Protect them aggressively.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-ast-global-color-1-color has-text-color has-link-color wp-elements-17">3. Benchmark Before Migration&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Take snapshots of:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Traffic&nbsp;</li>



<li>Rankings&nbsp;</li>



<li>Conversions&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-ast-global-color-1-color has-text-color has-link-color wp-elements-18">4. Use Staging Environment&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Never migrate directly&nbsp;on&nbsp;live&nbsp;site.&nbsp;</p>
