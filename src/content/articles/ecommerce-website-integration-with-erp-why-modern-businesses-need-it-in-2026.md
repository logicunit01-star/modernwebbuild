<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Running an ecommerce business today is more complicated than simply launching a website and waiting for orders. Online stores now manage inventory across multiple channels, process hundreds of customer interactions, handle logistics, update financial records, and synchronize operations in real time.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Without a connected system, businesses quickly face problems like:&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Inventory mismatches&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Delayed order fulfillment&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Accounting errors&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Poor customer experience&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Manual data entry&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Scaling limitations&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is where ecommerce website integration with ERP becomes essential.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system connects your ecommerce platform with critical business operations like inventory, finance, CRM, supply chain, warehouse management, and reporting.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Instead of juggling disconnected software tools, ERP integration creates a centralized operational system that automates workflows and improves business efficiency.&nbsp;</p>
